Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Your privacy is central to the JP-Club learning experience. This policy explains how we handle your data.

📦

1. Information We Collect

JP-Club is designed to be lightweight and privacy-first. We primarily collect:

  • Local Storage Data: Your theme preferences (light/dark), furigana display settings, language selection (EN/BN), and lesson progress are stored locally on your device via browser `localStorage`. This data never leaves your device.
  • Usage Analytics: We may use anonymous, aggregated third-party analytics (e.g., GitHub Pages traffic insights) to understand platform usage and improve our curriculum. No personally identifiable information (PII) is tracked.
🎯

2. How We Use Your Data

Any data retained locally is used exclusively to personalize your learning experience. This includes resuming your exact position in the Minna no Nihongo lessons, maintaining your spaced-repetition flashcard intervals, and preserving your UI preferences across sessions.

🔒

3. Data Security

Because your progress and preferences are stored locally in your browser, you maintain full control over your data. We do not maintain a central user database or require account registration, eliminating the risk of large-scale data breaches. Clearing your browser cache will reset your local progress.

🔗

4. Third-Party Services

JP-Club is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's standard privacy policy applies to the hosting infrastructure. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

🛡️

5. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to control your data. Since JP-Club operates client-side, you can exercise your rights by:

  • Adjusting your preferences directly in the platform settings.
  • Clearing your browser's `localStorage` and cookies for this domain, which will permanently erase your local progress and reset the app to its default state.
  • Contacting us at the email below if you have specific privacy concerns or requests.
